# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Surfaces

Homepage (`index.html`). Persuade. Dark lifestyle hero only. White remainder. Sora throughout. Steel flag in nav.

Base styles are phone-first. Complexity is added at 768px and 1024px. Touch targets are 44px. Hover scale is gated to fine pointers.

## Palette

- Night `#141618` hero film and drawer
- Paper `#ffffff` remaining sections
- Mist `#eef0f2` premium banner
- Ink `#1b1d20`
- Mute `#4e555c`
- Steel `#3d6f8c` premium nav flag and focus
- Line `#d5d8dd`

No cream, no brass. Radius 0.

## Type

Sora 400/500/600 for all text, including titles. Display tracking about `-0.03em`. Body measure under 48ch.

## Layout

1. Lifestyle film hero, copy low-left, Shop watches
2. Origin: copy only. No studio pedestal.
3. Mid banner (collection), highlights carousel, mid banner (steel)
4. Radiante / steel callout with specs
5. Three live reviews
6. Reels stories tray, newsletter, closing banner, footer

## Motion

Hero copy straps in (clip and blur) on load. Lifestyle banners ease from a tighter crop as they enter. Nav, buttons, Discover, reels, newsletter, and footer links answer the pointer with fill, underlines, and crop, not bounce. Hero stills crossfade until a lifestyle mp4 is supplied. Highlights and reels step. Nav lightens after the hero. All gated by `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Chrome

Nav 56px / 64px. Search, account, cart always visible. Menu on small screens. No header Shop CTA. Stainless steel is a Men submenu item plus a top-level Steel link.
