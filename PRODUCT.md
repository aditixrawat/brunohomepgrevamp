## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (user-selected)

## Users

People shopping premium fashion watches for themselves or as gifts, mostly in India, comparing look, finish, and price before buying online.

## Product Purpose

Bruno Milano sells men's and women's fashion timepieces at honest mid-range prices. Success is a visitor understanding the offer, trusting the craft, and opening a collection or product page to buy.

## Positioning

Timeless design, honest craftsmanship, and fair prices together. Quality and honest pricing are not a trade-off. Quality timepieces, accessibly priced. Made for the wrist, not the museum. Competes in premium fashion-watch space (Nixon, MVMT), not haute horology.

## Operating Context

Shopify storefront. Primary conversion is collection and product pages. Warranty is registered online (one year). Shipping is pan-India, typically 24h dispatch. 7-day returns.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Japanese quartz movement, sapphire-coated glass, 1-year warranty, free shipping across India (from live store copy).
- Founded in 2024; founders describe 30+ years of fashion-watch manufacturing expertise in the category, not a 2024 founding of the factory itself.
- This repo is a homepage prototype. Links should point at live collection URLs on brunomilano.com.
- Do not invent prices, discounts, customers, or engineering claims beyond what the live site states.
- Radiante Heritage lists at Rs 4,499 on the live store (41 mm, leather strap). Do not claim full sapphire crystal unless a PDP states it. Store-wide glass claim is sapphire-coated.
- H. Moser close-up luxury treatment is rejected for the first fold. Premium fashion, not luxury: lifestyle in life, never material macros as the lead.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Bruno Milano
- Voice: direct, unsentimental, quality-first. Premium, not luxury. “I definitely want the site to feel premium. Do not want it to feel luxury.”
- Homepage flow (user-binding, Sep 2026):
  1. Hero lifestyle video (not macro product)
  2. Brand story (mission, history, humanizing)
  3. Featured / bestseller slider: name + button only
  4. Premium collection callout (Radiante Heritage / steel line)
  5. Reviews
  6. Instagram grid that actually works
- White page throughout. Dark full-page limited to the hero video. Sans-serif throughout (Sora). Tighter spacing than a gallery layout.
- Nav must surface stainless steel under Men, plus Chronograph. Premium steel gets a distinct type treatment, not a sale-red gimmick.

## Product Principles

- Fashion-watch first. The wrist in life, not the loupe.
- Make the steel line findable. Do not bury it in Shop All.
- Keep copy short. Specs for premium SKUs must be readable without hunting.
- Preserve live-site facts; never invent luxury-house heritage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on body and controls. Honor prefers-reduced-motion. Keyboard-accessible slider, video, and menus.
